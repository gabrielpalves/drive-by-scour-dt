\section{Limitations and practical feasibility}
\label{sec:limitations}

The evidence is numerical and conditional on the model and the benchmark
design. All responses come from a two-dimensional TTBI model with vertical and
pitch dynamics only, so lateral, torsional, and soil--structure interaction
effects are absent. Each support is represented by a single vertical spring:
the scour scenarios are reductions of support stiffness, not scour depths, and
no hydraulic-to-stiffness relation is implied. The wheel--rail contact is
linear; it is also bilateral, although no tensile contact force occurred in any
passage. The track model inherits simplified planar conventions,
with mixed per-seat and two-rail parameter scaling, ballast mass lumped at the
deck nodes, and no shear coupling between ballast blocks. Because the Rayleigh
coefficients are recomputed for each bridge configuration, the stiffness
changes that represent damage also modify the damping matrices. The three
damage types are design coordinates of the benchmark --- a 5\% or 10\% loss of
support stiffness, a $10^{9}$~N\,m/rad restraint of the entrance bearing, and a
14\% or 22\% loss of $EI$ over 0.30~m --- not calibrated field quantities, and
the crack is a uniform stiffness reduction rather than a breathing or tapered
crack.

The benchmark also restricts the scope of the conclusions. One rail-profile
realization is shared by all passages, so the profile is not a source of
variability, and the ranking of the channels, in particular the near-chance
accuracy of the bogie pitch rates, may change under profile variation. The ten
scenarios are fixed configurations and the split is made at the passage level:
the accuracies measure the recognition of known configurations under new
operational conditions, not the generalization to unseen severities, crack
locations, or bridges. The speed, temperature, and vehicle-property ranges are
those of the source study rather than measured route or fleet distributions,
and the uniform 5\% noise of Eq.~\eqref{eq:noise} is not a model of a specific
sensor.

The statistical scope is equally limited. The 20-run statistics describe the
variability caused by network initialization for one data partition, not the
uncertainty over datasets or structures. Comparisons between arms involve
different parameter counts, independent 100-proposal searches, and the
1,000-epoch limit, so an accuracy difference does not isolate a single
mechanism. The published reference accuracies are best-run values, so they
are compared with the best of the present runs rather than with their means.
The control with the published finalists reproduced two Keras defaults, the
initialization and the Adam update, on the assumption that the source used
them; it did not reproduce the Keras implementations of the learning-rate
schedule and early stopping or the numerical defaults of TensorFlow, and it
cannot separate the simulator from the other unreported details of the source.
Finally, all configurations were compared on the same test set, so the best
configurations were identified after the test results were known and their
rankings are descriptive.

Field application requires several further steps. The carbody pitch rate can be
measured with a gyroscope, such as those of common inertial measurement units,
and carbody-mounted equipment is exposed to the least severe vibration category
of the railway standard IEC~61373~\cite{en61373}, which makes this response
attractive in practice. A hardware-specific observation model is nevertheless
needed to represent sensor noise, bias, bandwidth, and mounting. The spatial
resampling requires the speed and position of the train, and supervised
classification requires labelled data or a credible route through model
updating or domain adaptation. Transfer across vehicles, rail profiles, bridge
types, and speeds outside 70--90~km/h remains untested. A staged pathway would
begin with instrumented crossings of healthy bridges to calibrate the
environmental, operational, and observation models, followed by controlled
stiffness interventions or monitoring of scour-critical
structures~\cite{malekjafarian2015review, fernandes2026canelas}.
