\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper reconstructed the ten-class drive-by multi-damage benchmark of
Fernandes et al.~\cite{fernandes2025early}, training every configuration with
20 random initializations, and extended it to six responses of the leading
vehicle, 15 two-channel inputs, and a CNN with multi-rate pooling. The main
conclusions are as follows:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The published best-run accuracies were not recovered. With the two
  source channels, the best of 20 runs reached at most 71.8\% for the carbody
  and 73.8\% for the front bogie, against published values of up to 86.7\% and
  82.2\%, and the validation score used for tuning exceeded the subsequent test
  mean by 10.5 percentage points on average. The benefit of PAA for the carbody
  channel and the confusion between DC9 and DC10 were reproduced qualitatively.
  Drive-by classifiers should therefore be reported with their complete run
  distributions, including collapsed runs, rather than with their best run.
  \item The carbody pitch rate, which the source study did not use, was the
  most informative response: it had the highest median accuracy in all six
  combinations of network and tuning strategy and the highest mean in five. The
  bogie pitch rates stayed near the chance level. Both findings are consistent
  with the vehicle geometry: the 16~m bogie base of the carbody is comparable to
  the 19.95~m spans, whereas the 2.3~m wheelbase of a bogie is not.
  \item With channel-specific tuning, PAA with multi-rate pooling on the
  carbody pitch rate reached a mean accuracy of 77.63\% with a standard
  deviation of 2.24 percentage points, no collapsed run, and 43,642 parameters.
  Its advantage over the flatten-based CNNs on the same channel lies mainly in
  stability: the median gain over tuned PAA-CNN was 1.45 points, and the
  flatten-based networks were still improving at the 1,000-epoch limit.
  \item Bearing damage and scour level were recognized in 99.97\% and 97.63\% of
  the test passages, whereas the crack label limited the accuracy (77.76\%):
  all major confusions involved scenarios that differ only in the crack.
  \item With shared hyperparameters, two-channel inputs did not systematically
  outperform the better of their two single channels; among the best pairs,
  the only consistent gain was obtained by the rear-bogie vertical acceleration
  combined with the carbody pitch rate in PAA-MR (4.65 points, higher accuracy
  in 15 of 20 paired runs).
\end{enumerate}

These conclusions are conditional on one simulated bridge, one rail profile,
and a passage-level split. Future work should retrain the published finalist
architectures on the present data to apportion the reconstruction gap, vary
the rail profile and the operational ranges, estimate support-stiffness loss
as a continuous quantity on unseen damage states, and validate the pitch-rate
response with instrumented trains.
